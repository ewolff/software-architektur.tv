# 🚀 Surviving the AI-Native Era: From Pull Requests to Personal IDEs

What does the future of software development look like when AI enters the game? In this eye-opening episode of Software-Architektur im Stream, we dive deep with Sander Hoogendoorn into how teams are fundamentally rethinking their entire approach to building software.

**Key takeaways:**

✨ **Trunk-based development** over pull requests: Shorter feedback loops = faster iteration and more business alignment

🤖 **AI isn't just about speed** – it's about doing things that were previously impossible (Sander built a custom table component in 3 days that would have taken weeks before)

👨‍💻 **The "Product Engineer" concept**: Direct collaboration between developers and business stakeholders, eliminating intermediaries and reducing decision-making overhead

📊 **SaaS disruption is real**: Invoice recognition, translation tools, project management platforms – many are becoming commodities that teams can build themselves in days

⚖️ **The architect's new role**: Ensuring enterprise-grade safety, security, scalability, and domain coherence – while everyone else builds prototypes

🔄 **Agile isn't dead** – it's evolving faster than ever. The frameworks are outdated, but the core principle remains: continuously uncover better ways of working.

But here's the challenge: **How do you balance enablement with safety?** Sander shares how they're building sandboxes for non-technical stakeholders to create software without breaking production.

Tune in to hear why automation and smart architecture matter more than throwing people at problems – and what skills will actually remain relevant in the AI era.
