🏗️ **What is Software Architecture REALLY About?**

In a thought-provoking discussion with James Coplien (aka "Cope"), we challenge conventional wisdom about dependencies, modularity, and the true nature of software architecture.

Key insights from this deep dive:

🎯 **Managing dependencies is a doomed approach** – Everything depends on everything. Rather than obsessing over coupling metrics, we should focus on understanding the essential complexity of our domain.

👥 **Architecture is fundamentally about PEOPLE** – Not tools, patterns, or frameworks. It's about teams building shared mental models and "theory" about their systems over time.

📚 **Code is a liability; theory is the asset** – The knowledge your team holds matters infinitely more than the code artifacts you produce.

🔄 **Stable teams > Dependencies management** – The path to maintainability isn't better abstractions or message buses. It's teams that stay together long enough to truly understand their domain.

💡 **The hard truth:** Most software architecture talk is wishful thinking. Real solutions require organizational culture, domain expertise, and teams committed to deep, long-term mastery.

This challenges everything you thought you knew about microservices, design patterns, and API design.
