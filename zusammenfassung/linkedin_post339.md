# 🎨 The Hidden Power of Modern CSS – And Why It's About More Than Just Styling

Just had an incredible conversation with **Dylan Beattie** about CSS, and it's blown my mind how much this technology has evolved – and how often we're solving problems the hard way.

## Key Takeaways:

✨ **CSS has become insanely powerful** – what took weeks to build (remember nine-slice rounded buttons?) is now literally 2 lines of code. Liquid glass effects, masonry layouts, container queries – it's all native now.

⚙️ **It's also a software architecture problem** – CSS maintainability isn't about aesthetics, it's about dependency management, deprecation, and knowing when to delete things. Spoiler: most of us don't.

🤖 **The AI conversation matters differently here** – Using LLMs for pipeline dependencies (building tools) vs. production (serving users) is NOT the same. Know the difference.

🔥 **Utility classes aren't always the answer** – Neither is CSS-in-JS. The real question: do you have a CSS architect on your team? (Most companies don't, and it shows.)

🌍 **The web's business model is fundamentally broken** – But there are green shoots of change. Local restaurants building their own systems instead of paying 30% to delivery apps.

## The Real Talk:

We spend so much time arguing about frameworks that we've forgotten: **the browser can do SO much now**. Before reaching for the next library, check what native CSS and HTML can already do. You might be surprised.
