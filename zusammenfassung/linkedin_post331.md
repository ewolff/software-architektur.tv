# 🚀 Building Software in the Age of AI: Key Insights from Randy Shoup

Just wrapped an incredible conversation with Randy Shoup (Head of Engineering at CircleCI) on how AI is fundamentally reshaping software architecture and development practices.

Here are the game-changers:

**The Real Engineering Problem Isn't Coding—It's Validation**
AI can generate code at machine speed. The challenge? We need to evaluate it at machine speed too. This means engineering robust harnesses, evaluations, and testing pipelines—not just writing more code.

**Industrialization is Coming to Software**
Like semiconductors and pharmaceuticals, software development is moving from craft to industrial scale. That means standardized evaluation frameworks, deterministic checks, and automated validation. The engineering challenge has shifted upstream (specs) and downstream (verification).

**Architecture Matters More Than Ever**
Modularity, clear specifications, static analysis, test-driven development—the practices we've known for decades aren't old news. They're *essential* when AI produces code at scale. You can't rewrite everything from scratch.

**The 9x Difference**
Companies moving 9x faster than average? They're not writing more tests. They're architecting their pipelines to reduce friction, moving evaluations left (inner loop), and building platform teams that engineer the harness itself.

**Key Takeaway:** Your role is evolving from *programmer* to *developer*—from typing code to solving problems, defining specs, and architecting systems that can safely leverage AI at scale.
