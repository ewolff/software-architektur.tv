In this special episode of Software-Architektur im Stream and
maschinenraum.fm, host Lucas Dohmen sits down with web development veteran
Dylan Beattie to explore the surprising power of modern CSS.

Long dismissed as a mere styling tool, CSS has evolved into a robust
language for layout, animation, accessibility, and complex interactions —
often eliminating the need for heavy JavaScript frameworks.

Drawing on decades of experience building for the open web, Dylan shares
how combining semantic HTML with contemporary CSS can simplify front-end
architecture, reduce dependency on frameworks like React, and help
developers ship faster and more maintainably.

Tune in to discover how web development is being reimagined.
